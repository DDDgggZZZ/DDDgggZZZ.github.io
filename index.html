Guangze Deng 邓广泽
CS Undergraduate · Class of 2027
Hunan University · Changsha, China

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Hunan University, working with Prof. Changjian Chen. My research lies at the intersection of Visual Analytics and Human-Centered AI. I am interested in building and evaluating interactive systems that help people understand, evaluate, and steer AI for data analysis, with a particular focus on LLM- and agent-based workflows.

Beyond research, I play Go (amateur 5-dan), ride bikes around Hunan, chase mountain light with a camera, and try to write code that ages well.
